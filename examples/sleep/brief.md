# Brief — sleep

- **Example name:** sleep
- **Style:** editorial · **Canvas preset:** story (1080×1920)
- **Brief:** "why sleep matters, for a health blog"
- **Data** (commonly cited public-health figures — cited as "commonly cited
  figures (CDC et al.) — illustrative" in the footer):
  - Adults need 7–9 h; about 1 in 3 adults report getting less than 7
  - Sleep stages per night: ~50% light · ~25% REM · ~25% deep
  - After an all-nighter, reaction times are comparable to 0.10% blood alcohol
  - What helps: consistent schedule, cool dark room, no screens 1 h before bed

Only this data is used; rephrased but no new statistics.

## Story

One sentence: **sleep is a 7–9 hour nightly requirement that a third of
adults shortchange — with impairment comparable to being drunk.**
Hero: the towering "7–9" hours numeral. Support: stage donut (moon),
1-in-3 pictogram, BAC pull-quote, three-habit charticle.

## Design decisions

- Composition pattern: **Editorial spread** — thin left rail anchored by a
  rotated Fraunces 900 numeral, 2/3 content column; full-width pull-quote
  and charticle bands below; the stage donut crosses the column rule.
- Visual metaphor: **the moon** — the sleep-stage donut is drawn as a
  night-sky ring with an engraved crescent moon in its hole; sleeping-figure
  beds carry the 1-in-3 count; engraved stars in the empty zone.
