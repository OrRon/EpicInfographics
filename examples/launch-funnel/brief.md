# launch-funnel

- **Style:** dark-glass
- **Canvas preset:** square (1080×1080)
- **Brief:** "our signup funnel for the growth deck"
- **Footer:** fictional demo data — made with epic-infographics

## Data (fictional product)

| Stage | Users | Stage-to-stage rate |
|---|---|---|
| Visitors | 84,000 | — |
| Signups | 12,600 | 15% of visitors |
| Activated | 6,900 | 55% of signups |
| Paying | 2,100 | 30% of activated |
| Retained at 6 months | 1,600 | 76% of paying |

The stage-to-stage drop-offs are the story; funnel widths must be
proportional to the real values. Use only this data — rephrase freely,
never add new statistics.

## Story (one sentence)

84,000 people arrive and only 1,600 are still here at month six — but the
funnel's strongest wall is the last one: 76% of paying users stay.

## Design decisions

- **Composition pattern:** Big Object — a luminous beam-funnel dominates the
  left ~60% of the canvas and bleeds off the top edge.
- **Visual metaphor:** a beam of launch light narrowing through the darkness
  into a single retained star. The beam IS the funnel chart: every band's
  width is computed as value ÷ 140 px, so the geometry is arithmetically
  truthful.
- **Hero:** the 1,600 retained users (gradient hero number, double glow;
  the star at the beam's tip echoes it).
- **Support:** in-funnel stage labels with leader lines, "×0.15"-style
  multiplier annotations at each contraction, and a scanline glass panel of
  four conversion progress meters (a second, rate-focused reading of the
  same four given percentages).
