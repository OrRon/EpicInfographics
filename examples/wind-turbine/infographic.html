typical 4 MW onshore machine — figures rounded
1 · Hub & spinner — the three blades bolt on here. The whole rotor turns a lazy ~15 rpm.
2 · Main shaft — slow but immensely strong: all the rotor's torque flows through this one steel bar.
3 · Gearbox — steps ~15 rpm up about 100× to ~1,500 rpm — the speed the generator needs.
4 · Generator — spins at ~1,500 rpm and makes up to 4 MW of electricity.
5 · Weather mast — wakes the turbine at ~11 km/h of wind, parks it for safety at ~90.
6 · Yaw ring — motors swivel the whole head so the rotor always faces the wind.
7 · The climb — a ladder runs ~100 m up inside; the cables ride down with it.
8 · Foundation — a buried concrete anchor holds it all upright.
Each blade ≈ 63 m — tips travel up to ~290 km/h in a working breeze.
