# pizza-anatomy — brief

- **Brief:** "the anatomy of a perfect margherita"
- **Style:** hand-drawn · **Canvas:** square (1080×1080)
- **Footer:** "one cook's opinion — illustrative"

## Data (illustrative spec — use only this, rephrasing allowed, no new statistics)

- Dough: 65% hydration, 48–72 h cold ferment, 250 g ball
- Bake: 450 °C, 90 seconds, wood-fired
- Build ratio by weight: crust 55% · sauce 20% · fior di latte 20% · basil + oil 5%
- Six parts to label on the pizza: cornicione, crumb, char spots, sauce, cheese, basil

## Design decisions

- **Story:** a perfect margherita is mostly craft in the dough and fire — the toppings are a rounding error.
- **Composition pattern:** Big Object — a large top-view drawn pizza (~64% of canvas width) bleeding off the right edge, with the six parts pinned by doodle arrows. A magnifier bubble at bottom-left zooms into the cornicione cross-section so "crumb" can be labeled honestly.
- **Data forms:** annotated object (6 labels) · liquid-fill measuring cup (65% hydration) · stat scribbles (48–72 h, 250 g) · circled hero number on a washi-taped note (90 s at 450 °C, wood-fired) · single stacked bar with truthful widths (55/20/20/5).
- **Hero:** the pizza itself; secondary hero: "90 sec" circled in crayon red.
