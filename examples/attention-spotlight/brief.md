# Attention spotlight — brief

**Story (one sentence):** In twenty years the spotlight of your uninterrupted
attention has shrunk to less than a third of its 2004 size — 47 seconds is all
that's left before something cuts in.

**Hero:** the 2023 figure — 47 seconds — as a giant gradient number beside the
smallest, brightest pool of light with a lone figure standing in it.

**Where is the reader standing?** Seated in the dark of a theatre — we look
over the silhouetted seat backs and heads of the audience, past the footlights,
at a stage where the spotlight of focus has been contracting act by act.

**Metaphor:** the spotlight pool IS the chart. A lighting truss at the top
carries three fixtures; each throws a beam down the canvas to a pool of light.
Pool AREA is truly proportional to seconds of unbroken focus
(area ∝ s ⇒ rx ∝ √s, constant ellipse aspect — arithmetic in the HTML comment).

**Canvas:** story 1080×1920. **Style:** dark-glass (validated slots, aurora +
constellation, glow on hero only, mono data labels).

**Composition pattern:** Diagonal drive — the three acts step down-right in
three beats (2004 upper-left, 2012 centre, 2023 lower-right on the stage
floor). Truss bleeds both edges; Act I pool bleeds the left edge; a glass tag
crosses beam III; seats bleed the bottom. Dense zone: the stage/bottom third.
Empty zone: deep darkness mid-right.

**Data mapping (only the given figures):**
- 150 s (2004) / 75 s (2012) / 47 s (2023) → three proportional-area light
  pools, values in mono inside the pools, act tags as small glass chips with
  leader lines. Derived phrasing only ("halved", "less than a third").
- ≈23 min to refocus → staged ON beam III: a pink notification "ping" ripple
  hits the beam and a dashed detour path wanders off into the dark and back
  to the pool, labelled "≈23 MIN to fully refocus after one interruption".
- ≈77 email checks/day → the footlights: 77 amber bulbs along the stage lip,
  one per check, direct-labelled.

**Forms used:** proportional shapes (hero chart) · big-number callout (47 s) ·
path-as-story (detour arc) · pictogram count (77 footlight bulbs).
