Seven stops · 1969 → 2022

The road the
internet took

Every stop is a tiny world the web once lived in — follow the dashed road downhill from a machine room to everyone's pocket.

1969
ARPANET says «LO»

The first network message was meant to be "LOGIN" — the system crashed two letters in.

1989
The Web is proposed

Tim Berners-Lee pitches a "mesh" of linked documents at CERN. His boss: "vague, but exciting."

1993
Mosaic goes visual

The first popular graphical browser puts images next to text — the web gets a face.

1998
Google is founded

Two students organize the web from a garage — search becomes the front door.

2004
The social era begins

Facebook launches from a dorm room; the web learns your name.

2007
The web goes mobile

The iPhone puts the whole internet in a pocket, and the road never looks back.

2022
Chat with a machine

ChatGPT reaches 100 million users in two months — the fastest-adopted app in history at the time. The crowd is still arriving.
