# web-history-road — brief

- **Brief:** "the road the internet took" — a winding road through a miniature
  isometric landscape, one tiny landmark scene per milestone (redo of the
  rejected flat card-timeline).
- **Style:** isometric-world · **Canvas:** story (1080×1920)
- **Scene:** the reader hovers over a chain of seven floating slab-worlds,
  descending the canvas from 1969 to 2022, linked by a dashed road.
- **Footer:** "well-known public milestones · stops evenly spaced — intervals
  not to scale"

## Milestones (well-known public facts; nothing added)

1969 ARPANET's first message (crashed after "LO") · 1989 Berners-Lee proposes
the WWW · 1993 Mosaic makes the web visual · 1998 Google founded · 2004
Facebook launches · 2007 iPhone puts the web in every pocket · 2022 ChatGPT
reaches 100M users in 2 months

## Design decisions

- Composition: Big Object / story path — seven slabs in an S, dashed road
  connecting exit vertex → entry vertex; painter's order top-to-bottom so
  lower worlds overlap in front. Sky (clouds, balloon, bird) = empty zone.
- Landmarks (2:1 iso, X = X0+(x−y)·0.866, Y = Y0+(x+y)·0.5−z, one sun,
  face triads from the style): mainframe cabinet · CERN desk with memo ·
  CRT with browser window · garage · dorm tower with window grid · phone
  obelisk · glowing monolith with a small crowd (100M).
- Every year/title/caption on billboard tags stemmed to its slab,
  alternating sides.
