# honeybee-economy

- **Style:** retro-print · **Canvas preset:** a4 (1240×1754)
- **Brief:** "what one hive actually produces"
- **Footer:** "commonly cited beekeeping figures — illustrative"

## Data (use ONLY this; rephrase but never add statistics)

- One colony ≈ 50,000 bees in summer · one bee makes ≈ 1/12 teaspoon of honey in its lifetime
- ≈ 2 million flower visits per pound of honey · a hive collectively flies ≈ 88,000 km per pound
- ~1/3 of food crops depend on pollinators
- The cast: 1 queen · ~300 drones · the rest are workers

## Design decisions

- **Story:** one bee's lifetime output is almost nothing (1/12 tsp) — the hive's
  scale (50,000 bees, 2M flower visits, 88,000 km per pound) is what actually produces.
- **Hero:** giant "1⁄12 teaspoon" with a drawn teaspoon and a tiny honey drop.
- **Metaphor / Big Object:** a drawn straw skep hive (sunburst behind), annotated;
  a dashed flight path leaves the hive entrance and carries the per-pound stats.
- **Composition pattern:** Big Object + path-as-spine; seal and flight path cross
  section boundaries; only the seal rotates.
- **Support forms:** annotated object (hive, 50,000), big-number callouts on the
  flight path (2M visits, 88,000 km), specimen strip (queen/drone/worker cast),
  circular seal carrying the 1/3-of-food-crops stat.
