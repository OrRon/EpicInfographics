## Story
One sentence: *a rocket is almost entirely propellant, burned in 480 seconds of
precisely-timed stages to reach 28,000 km/h.* Hero = the drawn vehicle itself
(Big Object); the 28,000 km/h number is the loudest single value, attached to
the orbit-insertion event.

## Metaphor
The rocket IS the chart: section cut-fill (hatch) encodes the ~90% propellant
mass fraction; a time-proportional vertical spine beside it carries the five
flight events (liftoff at the bottom, orbit at the top — altitude = progress);
a dashed flyback arc returns stage 1 to the ground with its ≈8 min value.

## Composition
Big Object (blueprint's native pattern). Dense zone: bottom (pad, liftoff,
thrust bar, title block). Empty zone: the mid-right "coast" between fairing
jettison and cutoff — true to the physics. Boundary crossings: exhaust plume
through the bottom border frame; trajectory centerline off the right edge.
