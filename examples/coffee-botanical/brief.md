# coffee-botanical — brief

**Style:** naturalist-plate · **Canvas:** a4 (1240×1754)

**Story (one sentence):** The most-collected specimen on Earth — humanity takes
2.25 billion cups of *Coffea arabica* every single day.

**Scene ("where is the reader standing?"):** leafing through a 19th-century
naturalist's folio of economic botany, open to Plate IX — an engraved,
hand-tinted study of the coffee tree with the collector's observations on its
consumption at the plate's foot.

**Composition pattern:** Big Object — one engraved flowering/fruiting branch
dominates the left half, full height, one leaf tip crossing the plate frame;
the plate chrome (double-rule frame, plate number, figure system, foot
captions) is the world-building.

**Hero:** the 2.25 billion figure, set as a giant Cormorant numeral beside the
drawn cup (Fig. 4), with "≈26,000 every second" as its italic aside.

**Data → drawn element map (only the supplied data; no invented statistics):**
- ~2.25 billion cups/day, ≈26,000/second → Fig. 4, hero numeral + engraved cup
- Per-capita kg/yr (FI 12.0 · NO 9.9 · IS 9.0 · DK 8.7 · NL 8.4) → Fig. 5,
  apothecary-style thin bars, watercolor tint + ink outline, scale bar 0–12 kg
- Brazil ≈ 1/3 of world production → Fig. 6, three jute sacks, one stamped
  BRAZIL; also echoed in the herbarium-tab annotation "gathered in Brazil…"
- ~2 in 3 U.S. adults drink daily → Fig. 7, three drawn cups, two tinted full,
  one empty
- Faux-taxonomic figures (style, not statistics): Fig. 1 branch with lettered
  part labels, Fig. 2 cherry in section, Fig. 3 the seed green & roasted

**Signature devices used:** plate header with Latin binomial; engraved specimen
with loose watercolor tints and hatched shading; figure system + caption strip;
foxing, vignette, grain at 0.2; herbarium paper tab on the stem; annotated
dimension ("twice the natural size" arrow under Fig. 2).

**Tension:** dense zone = the observations band at the foot; empty zone = paper
right of the cup and around the branch apex. Largest element (185px numeral) ≫
body 16px. Boundary crossings: leaf tip through the plate frame; a leaf tip
grazing the observations rule.

**Footer:** "commonly cited ICO · NCA estimates — illustrative".
