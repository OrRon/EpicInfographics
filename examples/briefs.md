# Test briefs — the example dataset

A standing set of briefs (with data) for exercising the skill. Use them to
test changes, demo styles, and populate the gallery. After any significant
change to the skill, render 3–4 of these fresh and inspect the results.
Rendered briefs live in their own `examples/<name>/` directory.

Data is either commonly-cited public figures (sourced in the brief) or
clearly-fictional illustrative data — never presented as fresh research.

---

## 1. coffee — "fun social post about coffee" ✅ rendered twice

v1: neo-brutalist card-grid (kept as the "before" of the anti-slop pass).
v2: retro-print, Big Object composition — the cup's fill level carries the
2-in-3 stat; misregistration, grain, sunburst, seal badge.

- ~2.25B cups consumed daily worldwide (≈26,000/second)
- Per-capita kg/yr: Finland 12.0 · Norway 9.9 · Iceland 9.0 · Denmark 8.7 · Netherlands 8.4
- Brazil grows ~1/3 of world production
- ~2 in 3 U.S. adults drink coffee daily
- Sources: commonly cited ICO/NCA estimates — illustrative

## 2. focus — "make the decline of attention spans feel serious"

Style: swiss · Canvas: square · Forms: massive hero number, column series, stat pair

- After an interruption it takes ~23 minutes to fully refocus

- Source: research by Gloria Mark (UC Irvine), commonly cited figures

## 5. sleep — "why sleep matters, for a health blog" ✅ rendered

Style: editorial · Canvas: story

- Adults need 7–9 h; ~1 in 3 adults report getting less than 7 (CDC, commonly cited)
- Sleep stages per night: ~50% light · ~25% REM · ~25% deep
- All-nighter ≈ reaction times comparable to 0.10% blood alcohol (commonly cited)
- Recommended: consistent schedule, cool dark room, no screens 1 h before bed
  (candidate forms: pictogram 1-in-3, donut of stages, step flow of tips)

Style: blueprint · Canvas: story
